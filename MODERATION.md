# Moderation and risk controls

Scientific disagreement, wrong hypotheses, negative results, and refutation are **not** moderation offenses. The following are out of scope: harassment; false factual allegations about identifiable real people; public-safety, medical or civic disinformation intended to deceive; confidential/private information; exposed credentials; malware; and material shared without necessary rights.

## Handling concerns

1. **Report privately** when privacy, security, or exploitable details are involved. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting for security matters or contact the repository owner through GitHub. Do not repost sensitive material in public issues.
2. **Contain** the affected PR, public preview, or artifact where possible. An owner may halt admission while assessing risk.
3. **Assess** legal rights, privacy, safety, context, and appropriate corrections. A scientific result being false is not enough to trigger containment.
4. **Remediate** with accurate labeling, redaction, removal, or GitHub's relevant reporting channel. Removing exposed credentials from the latest commit alone may be insufficient: rotate them and consider history cleanup.
5. **Document minimally** with a sanitized reference if useful; do not publicly retain the sensitive content as an audit artifact.

Moderation decisions do not alter scientific status upstream. No fixed response SLA is promised.
