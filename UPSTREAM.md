# Relationship to S.T.A.R.-Labs

**Authoritative upstream:** https://github.com/LcosmosS/S.T.A.R.-Labs

**Existing sandbox repository:** https://github.com/LcosmosS/S.T.A.R.-Labs-Sandbox

The sandbox was an independent archive of R&D before this policy scaffold. Its historical files are retained, with their original Git history, in place. This change **does not import upstream controlled evidence, registries, preregistrations, or independent-review status**. A historical use of terms such as validation/proof is not a current epistemic designation.

New software imported from upstream should have explicit source path, source commit SHA, license provenance, and any attribution required by the license. Keep source-code reuse distinct from research-evidence transfer.

### Candidate promotion packet

A sandbox artifact may motivate a separate upstream PR, referencing:
- SBX ID and exact sandbox commit;
- precise claim being proposed and non-claims;
- experiment inputs, source hashes/versions, parameters, method, and falsifiers;
- negative/counterexample results and unresolved limitations;
- the upstream prerequisites for controlled execution or evidence status.

**No CI automation, tag, PR merge, label, or artifact in this repository changes upstream scientific status.**
