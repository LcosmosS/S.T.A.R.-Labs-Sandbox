## Review obligations

Independent arithmetic and topology review must assess the definitions, assumptions, counterexamples and proof sketches. A physical extension requires its own consistent dynamics, coupling, units, recovery limits and independently testable predictions. An astronomical operational hypothesis still needs concrete provenance-verified inputs, a prospectively frozen map/statistic/effect size, valid arithmetic and cosmological nulls, leakage controls, controlled execution and independent confirmation under the Charter.

Publication does not authorize a sky overlay or relax quarantines. It does not alter the frozen [A01](../../preregistrations/EXP-MAP-A01/protocol.md), [A03](../../preregistrations/EXP-MAP-A03/protocol.md), [P0](../../preregistrations/P0-ANSATZ-001/protocol.md) or [P0/M1 independent review](../../research/gates/P0_M1_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) requirements. The Charter's canonical D/E/M/P/H/C/T/S categories govern interpretation of the manuscript.
